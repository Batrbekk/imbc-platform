# Brief: update the IMBC Platform architecture from v0.4 to v0.5

## Who this is for

An agent, or the architect, revising `IMBC-Architecture-v0.4.pdf` (“Архитектура платформы IMBC — v0.4”, 4 November 2026). This brief replaces `v0.5-architecture-update-brief.md`. Do not apply that file. It was written against specification v1.3 before 11 November 2026, and its model of the technical-requirements files is no longer the specification.

Version 0.4 matches specification v1.2 (3 November 2026). Version 0.5 matches specification v1.3 as revised on 11 November 2026. The stack of v0.4 stays. The subject model moves where this brief says so.

## Sources of truth

Read these before editing, in this order of authority:

1. `docs/technical-specification/specification.md`, technical specification v1.3, 11 November 2026. It defines what Phase 1 builds. Requirement IDs such as `FC-08`, `UPD-04`, and `IMBC-19` come from it.
2. `GLOSSARY.md`, the terms to use.
3. `docs/notes/2026-11-11/spec-changelog.md`, the record of what the 11 November meetings changed. The specification wins where a note and the specification differ.
4. The earlier brief, only as a list of what not to copy. Where it says “key technical requirements”, a file per major operator and product position, or a PDF opened beside a forecast line, the specification has replaced that.

The decision log and `docs/notes/2026-11-02/descoped.md` still describe the earlier model. Do not copy them over the specification.

## Rules for the edit

- **Write in Russian.** Keep the section numbering of v0.4, including section 23. Add a “What changed in v0.5” table in section 1. Leave the v0.4 change table in place as history.
- **The stack stays.** Kubernetes, Argo CD, Vault or OpenBao, Valkey, Keycloak, pg-boss, CASL, NestJS, and React with Vite stay.
- **Where v0.4 says it matches specification v1.2, say v1.3 of 11 November 2026.** Leave the v0.4 table’s own statement that v0.4 was written against v1.2.
- **Retarget requirement IDs.** v0.4 cites IDs from before the 11 November renumber, including IDs the current specification does not have. When a sentence cites an ID, use the ID in the 11 November specification for the same rule. If that rule has no ID, drop the citation. Do not keep a dead ID and do not invent one.
- **Use the glossary.** Say **summarized technical requirements (STR)** and **key technical requirements excerpts (KTRE)**. Do not say “key technical requirements”, “generalized file”, or “technical specification” for these PDFs. Say **unresponsive** for the second missed quarter. **Inactive** remains the account with no sign-in for three months. Say **e-Ondiris**, not e-Öndiris. Say **Observer** (наблюдатель). Say **SME**, **IMBC administrator**, and **IMBC general director**.
- **Do not add scope.** If something seems to need an architectural element that neither v0.4 nor this brief covers, add it to section 23 instead of designing it.

## Changes by section

### §1. Main decision and changes

1. State that v0.5 brings v0.4 into line with specification v1.3 of 11 November 2026. The stack stays.
2. Add a “What changed in v0.5” table with one row per subject below, naming the section and the source ID. The subjects are: STR and KTRE as their own section; the NDA at registration; update status and visibility; the e-Ondiris copy; due diligence and catalog visibility; tiers and AGAR or CDP; the observer’s company page; the dashboard drill-through; legacy fields; the site map.

### §2. Delivery boundaries and releases

1. In Release 2, replace “файлами обобщённых технических спецификаций” with the demand forecast, STR, and KTRE: import and publish the forecast, publish NDA versions, and upload STR and KTRE files (section 5.4, ADM-10, ADM-12 to ADM-15). Export subscriptions stay in Release 2.
2. STR and KTRE are not optional in Phase 1, and they cannot be downloaded or printed (FC-08). The forecast stays view, search, and filter only (FC-06).
3. Phase 1 includes tiers and an AGAR or a CDP record on a product (IMBC-19 to IMBC-25). Sending that recommendation to operators is not in Phase 1 (IMBC-26). Take CDP/AGAR out of the “not in Phase 1” cell, and name the recommendation and the questionnaire as what stays out.
4. Phase 1 does not write requirements for an AI assistant (section 14). The assistant itself stays a Phase 2 foundation, as v0.4 already has it.
5. An unresponsive profile stays in search and on the map (UPD-04). Hiding it is not in Phase 1.

### §3. Scale assumptions

1. On the forecast row, delete “~28 файлов спецификаций”. The specification gives the forecast as about 77,000 lines for groups A–D and about 8,000 for group E (FC-10). It does not give a count of STR or KTRE files. Do not write 16 to 19 positions, and do not write a file per operator.
2. Replace the quarter-mail peak of about 2,000 update-status emails in a day. At most 50 of NOT-12, NOT-15, and NOT-16 go out in a day; a notice that is due waits for the next day that still has room (NOT-31). The invitation of migrated manufacturers is a separate send and is not under that cap.
3. Availability stays as v0.4 states it: designed for 99.5 percent, achieved by whoever operates the platform. Cite NFR-03 and NFR-04.

### §4. System context

1. e-Ondiris is a daily pull into a local copy. The platform does not call e-Ondiris when a profile section opens (EXT-02).

### §6. Domain modules

1. Replace `TechSpecFile` as a file of an operator or of a commodity group. The record is a technical-requirements file: one commodity group, one product, and a kind, STR or KTRE. It does not belong to an operator. It is not opened from a forecast line. There is no column from `ForecastLine` to that file. A product may have neither file. IMBC keeps the list of products that have a file. The PDF is IMBC’s own text. The platform does not parse it into fields (FC-08, ADM-15).
2. `NdaAcceptance` records the profile, the time, the NDA version, and the IP address. It does not record a person’s name or position. Acceptance is at self-registration, or on first sign-in for a migrated manufacturer. One acceptance covers the detailed forecast, the STR, and the KTRE (FC-02).
3. Update status is active, outdated, or unresponsive. Unresponsive stays visible and sorts below active and outdated. The module does not hide a profile for update status. The quarter belongs to the profile. Returning from unresponsive is a confirmation of each section (UPD-04, UPD-05, NOT-31).
4. Add an AGAR or CDP record: one per product line, kind AGAR or CDP, status, report file, comment (IMBC-23, IMBC-24). Tier is derived. Tier 1 is verified. Tier 2 is site surveyed. Tier 3 is that product when the record is report issued or a later status. A profile that is not verified is not a tier. “Works with” is not a tier (IMBC-19 to IMBC-21).
5. The catalog shows a profile to operators and observers when it is verified and the due-diligence result is green or amber, at any update status. A red result stays off those views until an SME sets green or amber (IMBC-11, UPD-04).
6. e-Ondiris lives in the external-checks module as a stored copy, refreshed once a day with new and changed records. A profile reads the copy when it is created and when a later pull changes that BIN (EXT-02, MAN-25).

### §7. Data model

1. Replace the `TechSpecFile` row. Store commodity group, product, kind (STR or KTRE), and the file. Class F for the manufacturer who is verified and has accepted the current NDA. Operators and observers do not see these files. No link from a forecast line (FC-08).
2. Keep the legacy fields on `ImbcNote`, one field per internal column, yellow columns included: expression-of-interest email sent, confirmation of receipt, source, response to the expression of interest, summary of that response, ready for site visit, overall ranking, shortlisted for CDP / AGAR, LC/LPS, priority commodity, year added, CDP / AGAR status, CDP / AGAR execution, general comments, the folder link, and the e-Ondiris note. LC/LPS is not a workflow. Overall ranking stays a legacy note and is not shown as a rank (IMBC-06, MIG-09).
3. A finished CDP or AGAR in the workbook becomes an AGAR or CDP record on the product line the approved mapping names. When the workbook does not name the product, those columns stay on the note until IMBC names it (MIG-09, IMBC-23). Site-visit conducted and its dates stay survey records (MIG-08).
4. Due diligence stores the result green, amber, or red, and each check with its group, subject, Adata’s value, and mark (IMBC-07, IMBC-11). The check runs when the profile is created, on the same Adata read as the prefill, and again once a quarter from the last check. A BIN in a registration that never becomes a profile is not queried.
5. A contact whose email or phone matches another contact on the same profile is rejected and not saved (MAN-13).
6. Prefill follows MAN-06, MAN-07, and MAN-08. Where the company legal name, the kind of activity, the company phone, or the company contact email is returned by both e-Ondiris and Adata, e-Ondiris is kept. Headcount is entered by the manufacturer (MAN-17). The manufacturer cannot change the BIN (MAN-09). A pulled product is one line; the manufacturer does not add a second line for it (MAN-25).

### §8. Processes and states

1. Replace the path active → outdated → inactive, and replace hiding the profile. One ignored notice, plus seven days, makes the profile outdated. A second makes it unresponsive. The profile stays visible and sorts below the others. Verified is not removed. An expired certificate can set outdated and does not by itself set unresponsive (UPD-03, UPD-04, REV-10, NOT-31).
2. The NDA tick is on the registration form, and on first sign-in for a migrated manufacturer. The forecast and the technical-requirements section stay closed until the profile is verified and the current NDA is accepted. Publishing a new NDA version closes both until it is accepted again. Opening either section does not ask again (FC-01, FC-02, FC-03).
3. On the technical-requirements section, files are grouped by commodity group and then by product. A product may show an STR, a KTRE, both, or neither. The reader zooms and scrolls. There is no download and no print. A click on a forecast line does not open a file (FC-08).
4. From a verified profile the manufacturer can request a site survey, naming no product, and can request an AGAR for one product that has an STR. Both appear in the SME’s list and send no email. A product with no STR cannot be requested. Phase 1 does not send the recommendation to operators (IMBC-20, IMBC-25, IMBC-26).

### §9. Access and data protection

1. In class F, STR and KTRE files open from the technical-requirements section for a verified manufacturer who has accepted the current NDA. They cannot be downloaded or printed. Operators and observers never see them (FC-08, GEN-13).
2. On the manufacturer row, the closed actions include downloading or printing the forecast, the STR, and the KTRE.
3. An observer sees the dashboard, the manufacturer list, and the detailed profile, including the company phone and email from the prefill and the tier. An observer does not see the focal point, the list of tier 3 products, an AGAR or CDP report, the demand forecast, the STR, or the KTRE. An observer does not export, subscribe, call the pull API, or write (GEN-13, IMBC-22).
4. Operators see the tier and which product lines are tier 3. They do not see the AGAR or CDP report file (IMBC-22, IMBC-26).

### §10. Catalog and search

1. The dashboard stays the overview in OPR-31. Its indicators are OPR-32, including tier and the three update statuses. Commodity groups A–E stay. Outdated and unresponsive manufacturers stay on the map (OPR-33).
2. Choosing a figure or a category opens the manufacturer list with that filter already applied. The list keeps filters in the address (OPR-05, OPR-35).
3. Search includes unresponsive profiles and sorts them below active and outdated profiles (OPR-01).

### §13. External checks

1. e-Ondiris has no published API. The platform keeps a local copy and pulls new and changed records once a day. A failed daily pull is not a due-diligence finding (EXT-02, MAN-25).
2. The Adata check that sets the due-diligence result is the read at profile creation, then once a quarter. It is not run on every open of the profile (IMBC-07).

### §14. Frontend

Replace the screen list so it matches the site map below. Add the site map in this section, before the screen list, as a high-level map of areas. It is not a list of URLs and not a visual design. Write it in Russian. The areas and the moves are these.

**Before an account**

- Manufacturer registration: company name, BIN, company email, terms, personal-data policy, the current NDA, and “How did you hear about us?”. No password on the form (MAN-01).
- Set password from the email link. Sign in. Email code on a new device. Responsibility dialog on first sign-in. Forgot password. Language, remembered on the account.

There is no notification inbox.

**Manufacturer.** The profile is the entry.

- Profile, eight sections: main information, contacts, personnel, shareholders, products, production, certificates, documents. Completeness, send for review, unlock, and the status of each section and file sit on the profile.
- Update status, and the section-by-section confirmation that returns a profile from unresponsive.
- Own tier, the site-survey request, and the status of each AGAR or CDP. The AGAR request is on a product that has an STR.
- Operator requests, with one reply.
- Demand forecast: the table, search, and filters. Closed until the profile is verified and the current NDA is accepted. No download and no print. A line does not open a file.
- Technical requirements: commodity group, then product, then the STR or the KTRE in a viewer with zoom and scroll. Same gate as the forecast. No download and no print. The section says these files are IMBC’s texts.

**SME.** The review queue is the entry.

- Queue, filtered by manufacturer, status, assignee, and date received.
- Profile under review, with a decision on each section and file, and submit result. Pending changes are the same profile, limited to the changed sections.
- Search across all profiles, and export.
- On a profile: site survey, due diligence, IMBC notes, works-with and visit marks.
- Manufacturer requests: site survey, and AGAR. Both are in the SME’s list.
- IMBC requests and operator requests.

**IMBC administrator.** No manufacturer data, and no dashboard.

- Organisations and accounts, including observers. Block and unblock. Change a manufacturer’s login email.
- Review assignment. The row shows the manufacturer’s name, the status, and the assignee.
- Reference lists, including which certificate and document types are mandatory.
- NDA versions.
- Forecast import and publish.
- STR and KTRE files: upload, replace, remove. Each file is one product in one commodity group, and is either the STR or the KTRE.
- Organisation-chart sample.
- Pull API credentials: issue and revoke.
- Invite all migrated manufacturers, with the report.
- Account events in the audit log.

**IMBC general director.** Read only. The dashboard is the entry.

- Dashboard. A figure or a category opens the manufacturer list with that filter applied.
- Manufacturer list, then the profile, including IMBC records and which operators work with the manufacturer.
- Full audit log. Activity snapshots. Export of a search.
- Demand forecast and technical requirements, view only.

**Operator.** The dashboard is the entry.

- Dashboard, then the manufacturer list, then the company page. Filters stay in the address.
- Company page: the verified profile, files, tier, which products are tier 3, update status, and the company-page PDF. No AGAR or CDP report file.
- Works-with mark, visit mark, and notes.
- Operator request and IMBC request.
- Export of the current search. Subscriptions and their files. Status of the organisation’s pull API credential.

**Observer.** The dashboard is the entry. The path is dashboard, manufacturer list, company page.

- The company page includes the company phone and email from the prefill, and the tier. It omits the focal point and the list of tier 3 products.
- The company-page PDF is that same reduced page.
- No forecast, no technical requirements, no export, no subscriptions, no pull API, and no write.

On the manufacturer’s forecast screen, do not open a PDF beside the table. The technical-requirements viewer is its own area (FC-08). On the operator, observer, and director dashboard, choosing a figure or a category opens the manufacturer list with that filter already applied (OPR-35).
