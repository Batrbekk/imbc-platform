## Backend implemented

- Migration0025 creates eight normalized LPS tables, author/save timestamps, logical-deletion provenance and guarded references. Programme/country/status definitions are added by migration; the normal audited release loader supplies LC/LPS, Kazakhstan and four initial statuses with en/ru/kk labels. - SME company/entry CRUD persists current saved values immediately. Operator and director read/list/dashboard only; manufacturer, observer and administrator access is refused by the persisted actor and SQL policy. Administrator dictionary maintenance retains its existing separate permissions. There is no SME/operator LPS history endpoint; retention does not grant audit access.
- Companies are reusable across entries. Editing shared company context atomically stamps all affected active entries with automatic author/time and records their complete before/after values. SME business `last_updated_on` stays unchanged unless explicitly edited. Manual status changes have no transitions or workflow effects.
- Unknown optional facts remain null. Investment uses exact decimal strings; planned launch retains year, quarter/range or TBD precision. Confirmed child identities, grouped contacts/methods and locations keep stable display order. Wrong dictionary, foreign child and stale retired-reference substitutions are rejected.
- PostgreSQL filters/counts/facets select matched entry IDs before graph loading. Different dimensions use AND, multiple values use OR, city/region and type/group predicates share one child relationship. Graph queries fetch only the selected page/subject IDs in bounded batches. Distinct company counts and multi-region/group buckets are not summed into the total.
- Actor lock precedes the dictionary shared advisory lock, then LPS read/write advisory lock, company/entry/reference rows. Direct DML acquires the dictionary/LPS locks before row locks. This keeps count/page/reference metadata coherent and avoids the dictionary-delete/LPS-write cycle.
- Logical deletion retains children and complete audit facts; normal detail/list/dashboard exclude deleted entries. Company/dictionary deletion cannot erase dependent entries. Every child parent FK is immutable, including company-country and contact methods, so direct application SQL cannot move facts out of deleted history or leave an unnamed source contact without its only method.
- Sorting uses typed numeric/date/launch bounds and dictionary order. Region keys retain the saved location order and repeated regions; entirely missing lists stay last in either direction. Stable entry ID breaks ties.
